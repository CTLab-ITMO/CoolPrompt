## squad_v2

**need_change:** True

**change_rationale:** Existing knowledge lacks organized strategies and principles for effective context-based answering.

### strategies

- Identify key themes in the context to inform the answer.
- Extract relevant data points that directly address the question.
- Contextualize answers to align with the question's intent.
- Validate the reliability of the context before formulating an answer.

### principles

- Ensure clarity and conciseness in responses.
- Maintain objectivity by minimizing personal bias.
- Acknowledge uncertainties when context is ambiguous.

### evaluation_criteria

- Relevance of the answer to the posed question.
- Clarity and coherence of the response.
- Depth of understanding demonstrated in the answer.

### gap_hypotheses

- Insufficient context may lead to inaccurate answers.
- Overlooked nuances in the context could skew interpretations.
- Lack of structured approach may hinder consistency.
